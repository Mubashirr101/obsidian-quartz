## What's in here

- **Notes** on tools, languages, and concepts I've worked through
- **Write-ups** of problems I've solved (and some I haven't, yet)
- **References** I keep coming back to, collected in one place
- **Half-formed ideas** that might turn into something later

Nothing here is final. Notes get revised as I understand things better. Treat dates and versions as approximate, not authoritative.
