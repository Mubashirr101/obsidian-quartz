### 🧠 **Types of AI Agents**

1. **Simple Reflex Agents**
    
    - Act only on current perceptions.
    - No memory of past states.        
    - Example: A thermostat that turns heating on/off based on temperature.
        
2. **Model-Based Reflex Agents**
    
    - Maintain some internal state or memory.        
    - Can handle partial information.        
    - Example: A chatbot that remembers the previous message.
        
3. **Goal-Based Agents**
    
    - Make decisions to reach a specific goal.        
    - Perform planning and search.        
    - Example: A robot navigating a maze to reach an endpoint.
        
4. **Utility-Based Agents**
    
    - Consider different outcomes and choose the one with the highest utility (value).        
    - Example: A self-driving car choosing the safest and fastest route.
        
5. **Learning Agents**
    
    - Improve their performance over time.        
    - Use reinforcement learning, supervised learning, etc.        
    - Example: AlphaGo learning to play Go by playing millions of games.
        
6. **Autonomous Agents**
    
    - Operate with minimal human intervention.        
    - Make decisions in real time.
    - Example: AI-powered customer support bots, drone swarms.

### ⚙️ **Applications of AI Agents**

- **Virtual Assistants (e.g., Siri, Alexa)**    
- **Chatbots and Customer Support Agents**    
- **Game AI (NPC behavior, adaptive difficulty)**    
- **Financial Trading Bots**    
- **Robotics (e.g., warehouse robots)**    
- **Healthcare Assistants (diagnosis, monitoring)**    
- **AI Companions / Digital Humans**
    

---

### 🤖 **Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)**

In complex scenarios, multiple agents work together (or compete), forming a **multi-agent system**. These agents can cooperate, coordinate, or negotiate to solve distributed problems.

### 🔍 Related Concepts

- **Agent Architecture** – How an agent is designed (rule-based, learning-based, hybrid).
    
- **[[Environment Types]]** – Fully/partially observable, deterministic/stochastic, static/dynamic.
    
- **Embodied Agents** – Have a physical form (e.g., robots).
    
- **Software Agents** – Purely digital, used in systems, apps, games, etc.
